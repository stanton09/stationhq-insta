Got a fire extinguisher at home? Know how to use it before you need it. 🧯

Remember PASS: Pull the pin. Aim low, at the base of the fire. Squeeze the lever slowly and evenly. Sweep side to side.

Only fight it if the fire is small and contained, 911 has been called, and you have a clear way out. If not, get out and call 911 (U.S. Fire Administration).
