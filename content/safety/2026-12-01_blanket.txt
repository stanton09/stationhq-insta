Cold nights, electric blanket on. 🛏️

Never fold it while it's on, don't let anything sit on top of it (other blankets or pets included), and don't tuck it in. Folded or covered blankets can overheat, and tucking can pinch the heating wires.

Cracks or breaks in the wiring or plug, or dark, charred or frayed spots on the blanket? Throw it out (U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission).
