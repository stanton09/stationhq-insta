Cold mornings are here. 🚗

Never leave a car running in an attached garage, even with the garage door open. Put CO alarms on every level of the home and outside sleeping areas.

Carbon monoxide is colorless and odorless, and early symptoms feel like the flu without the fever (U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission).
