Furnace kicking on for the season? 🔥

Have heating equipment and chimneys cleaned and inspected every year by a professional, keep anything that can burn at least 3 feet from heat sources, and never use an oven to heat your home.

Heating is the second leading cause of home fires, with about 32,200 reported in 2021 (U.S. Fire Administration).
