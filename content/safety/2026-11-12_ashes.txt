Cleaning out the fireplace? 🔥

Ashes go in a metal can with a lid, and the can goes outside at least 10 feet from the house. Never a paper bag, a cardboard box, the garage or the deck.

And have the chimney inspected and cleaned every year (U.S. Fire Administration).
