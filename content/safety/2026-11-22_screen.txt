Fireplace going tonight? 🔥

Keep a glass or metal screen in front of it to stop sparks and embers, never burn paper or wrapping paper in it, and put the fire out before you go to bed or leave the house (U.S. Fire Administration).

Never use gasoline or charcoal lighter fluid to light or relight a fire. The vapors can explode (U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission).
