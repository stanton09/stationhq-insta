Keep a kid-free zone of at least 3 feet around the stove, turn pot handles toward the back so nobody bumps or grabs them, and if you leave the kitchen, turn the burner off.

Got a burn? Put it in cool water for 3 to 5 minutes right away (U.S. Fire Administration).
