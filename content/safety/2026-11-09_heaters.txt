Space heater season. 🔥

Give it 3 feet on every side, plug it straight into the wall (never a power strip or extension cord), and turn it off when you leave the room or go to bed.

Portable heaters cause just 3% of home heating fires, but 41% of the deadly ones (U.S. Fire Administration).
