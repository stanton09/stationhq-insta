This time of year we run a lot of these.

Keep anything that can burn at least 3 feet from a space heater. Plug it straight into the wall, not a power strip, and shut it off when you leave the room or go to bed.

Portable heaters cause just 3% of home heating fires, but 41% of the deadly ones, per the U.S. Fire Administration.
