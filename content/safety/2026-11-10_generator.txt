Power out? Before you fire up the generator. ⚡

Run it outside only, at least 20 feet from the house. Never in the garage, basement or on the porch, and an open door or window won't vent it.

About 85 people die each year from carbon monoxide from portable generators (U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission).
