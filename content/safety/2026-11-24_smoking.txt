Family and friends over for the holidays? Some of them smoke. 🚬

Always smoke outside and never in bed. Use deep, sturdy ashtrays, never toss butts into mulch, potted plants or dry grass, and don't smoke if you're sleepy or have been drinking.

Smoking is the number 1 cause of home fire deaths across the country (U.S. Fire Administration).
