After the snow stops, take a walk around the outside of the house. ❄️

Clear the snow away from the vents for your furnace, dryer, stove and fireplace so carbon monoxide doesn't build up inside (U.S. Fire Administration, U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission).

More than 150 people in the U.S. die every year from accidental non-fire carbon monoxide poisoning (U.S. Fire Administration).
