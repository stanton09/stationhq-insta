Dryers work overtime this time of year. 🧺

Clean the lint filter every load, make sure the vent hose behind it isn't crushed or kinked, and check that the outside flap opens when it runs.

Not cleaning is the #1 cause of dryer fires, and they peak in January (U.S. Fire Administration).
