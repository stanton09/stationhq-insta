# Captain Roy Brisket: art reference

Everything used in the Captain Brisket reels, kept in one place.

- `Roy_Approved_Master_Transparent.png`: the approved master. Attach it to every ChatGPT prompt as the style reference.
- `originals_chatgpt/`: the images exactly as they came from ChatGPT (keep these, never edit).
- `cutouts/`: characters with the background removed, ready to animate (roy_*, kevin_*, kevinhead_*, roy_texting_frames/).
- `backgrounds/`: firehouse rooms (bay, dayroom, office) and the whiteboard-erase frames (wb_0..5).
- `expressions/`: Roy's face set (x_neutral, x_mad, x_furious...) and the phone-call pose.
- `sounds/`: Pixabay sound effects (free license, no credit needed).
- `music/`: Kevin MacLeod tracks (incompetech.com, CC BY 4.0: credit him in the caption).

Characters: Capt. Roy Brisket (34 years on the job, deadpan), Kevin (the probie, too eager).
Tip for new ChatGPT art: ask for "plain flat background, no white outline around the character".
